## About

I am a Computer Science PhD student at Purdue University, advised by Prof. Vamsi Addanki in the STyGIANet group. My research interests lie in computer networks, distributed systems, and hardware–software co-design.

I study how application communication should be coordinated with dynamically reconfigurable network fabrics. My current work explores reconfigurable photonic interconnects, including the tradeoff between topology-reconfiguration overhead and multihop communication. More broadly, I am interested in scheduling, topology design, and workload-aware interconnects for emerging distributed systems.

Previously, I completed my MSE in Computer and Information Science at the University of Pennsylvania, where I worked with Prof. Vincent Liu on scheduling for LLM serving systems whose computation changes at runtime. I contributed to DREX, a dynamic rebatching system for early-exit inference, and developed ConFLExit, a confidence-guided scheduling mechanism, for my master’s thesis. Before Penn, I was a software engineer on ShopeePay’s core systems team at Shopee.
